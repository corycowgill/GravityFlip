How to Play

  • Goal: reach the glowing exit.
  • You can't jump. You flip gravity.
  • Mobile: swipe up / down / left / right.
  • Desktop: arrow keys or WASD.
  • Tap / Space: hold for slow-mo (later levels).
  • Spikes kill you. So do lasers. So does the void.
